About Me

Creative Mind
Behind the Edits.

Hi, I’m GowshaRaja aka Edwin, a video editor who enjoys turning everyday moments into engaging short-form content. I create personal Instagram Reels using CapCut Desktop, focusing on scene selection, pacing, music synchronisation, captions, text placement and smooth visual flow. I also develop content ideas, captions, hashtags and a consistent visual identity for my creative page.

I use my own laptop for editing. CapCut Desktop is my current editing tool, and I’m exploring Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and PowerDirector to build familiarity with professional editing workflows. My wider creative practice includes Canva, Adobe Photoshop and AI-assisted visual concept development. I’m open to learning, adapting to a team’s process and taking on new creative challenges.

Editing Choices, Made Visible.

Illustrative visual studies showing how selection, pacing and colour mood can shape a short-form edit.

Scene Selection

02 · Build a rhythm

Pacing & Transitions

Keep cuts purposeful. Use a clean cut when the idea changes, and a transition only when it supports the flow.

Colour & Atmosphere

Warm highlights and controlled contrast can support a calm, cinematic mood. Keep skin tones and detail natural.
