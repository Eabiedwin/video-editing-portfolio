About Me

Creative Mind
Behind the Edits.

Hi, I’m GowshaRaja aka Edwin, a video editor who enjoys turning everyday moments into engaging short-form content. I create personal Instagram Reels using CapCut Desktop, focusing on scene selection, pacing, music synchronisation, captions, text placement and smooth visual flow. I also develop content ideas, captions, hashtags and a consistent visual identity for my creative page.

I use my own laptop for editing. CapCut Desktop is my current editing tool, and I’m exploring Adobe Premiere Pro, PowerDirector and other professional workflows. My wider creative practice includes Canva, Adobe Photoshop and AI-assisted visual concept development. I’m open to learning, adapting to a team’s process and taking on new creative challenges.
